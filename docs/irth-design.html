Design system

Direction: majlis at dusk. Warm, unhurried, textile-informed. Reference points are a sadu-woven cushion, a sand wall at golden hour, and the deep green of a ghaf at the edge of a farm. Content is the hero — voices, faces, the tree.

Reem Kufi carries the personality because it should be the Arabic that feels authored, not a Latin display face doing heritage cosplay. Plex Sans Arabic covers both scripts from one skeleton, so a bilingual screen never looks like two designs stapled together. Plex Mono handles dana counts, times, and dates — tabular figures stop numbers jittering as they tick up.

Elder Mode

Type ramp ×1.35, min touch target 56px, one primary action per screen, no nested navigation. Set per member, not per device.

Motion

220ms, ease (.22,.72,.28,1). The tree is the only thing that animates for longer — 1.4s on level-up. Everything respects reduce-motion.

Radii & depth

Cards 22px, controls 14px, chips full. No drop shadows inside the app — separation comes from a 1px sand line. Shadow is reserved for sheets that sit above the world.

Iconography

1.7px stroke, rounded caps, 24px grid. Outline by default; filled only for the active tab. Never icon-only for a destructive or a Tier-3 action — those always carry a word.

C9 · D1 · E2 · E3 · F2 · F3 · G-series polish
Two things to settle this week

Confirm the SMAC theme in writing — the published one may still be sustainability, and §4 of the spec is written to score either way. And agree the DB schema on day one, then don't renegotiate it: one of you owns Roots, the other owns Bonds and Branches, and shared ownership of a schema mid-sprint is how two-person teams lose a week.

Say this out loud in the demo

"We used AI for language and judgement, and ordinary code for anything where being wrong matters. Scheduling, medication and escalation are deterministic by design."

Then point at E5. Most student teams put AI on everything and hope nobody asks; an engineering panel is listening for exactly this sentence, and the care screen is the proof that you meant it.
